A four-application suite covering supplier onboarding and procurement,
external-entity engagement and contractor management, unified behind a central
API gateway with Business Central as the system of record.

## The problem

The procurement portal originally ran on a replicated copy of ERP data — a
model that drifted, aged badly and widened the security surface. Every
external-facing application spoke to its own copy of the truth, and keeping
those copies in step was a standing cost.

## The approach

I architected and led the migration to a gateway API: a single authenticated
entry point with OAuth 2.0 token exchange, role-scoped access, circuit-breaker
resilience and response caching, delivered in phases against a live production
system.

## What it runs on

- **Business Central** as the system of record, with AL codeunits for
  procurement, store requests, petty cash and staff claims.
- **ASP.NET Core** gateway in front of the ERP, exposing role-scoped REST APIs.
- **React / TypeScript** portals for suppliers and external entities.
- **IIS** with ARR reverse proxy, URL rewriting and SSL for client-facing
  services.
